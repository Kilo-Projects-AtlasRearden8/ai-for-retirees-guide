Staying Connected with Free AI

Write emails, set up video calls, organize family messages, and share photos — using free AI tools and plain English.

What you will do

  • Write or tidy up emails to loved ones
  • Set up and join free video calls
  • Create group messages for family updates
  • Draft invitations and thank-you notes
  • Turn photos and memories into simple captions and stories

What you need

  • A computer, tablet, or smartphone with an internet connection
  • A web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox)
  • A free email address (Gmail, Outlook, or similar)
  • A free account with an AI tool (for example, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or Claude — pick whichever is easiest for you)

Step-by-step checklist

Part 1: Write your first email with AI

  • Open your email inbox and start a new message
  • Open your AI tool in another browser tab
  • Type a request like: “Help me write a friendly email to my granddaughter inviting her to visit for Thanksgiving. Keep it short and warm.”
  • Copy the AI's draft and paste it into your email
  • Add your own personal details (names, dates, inside jokes)
  • Read it out loud before you send
  • Send the email

Part 2: Set up a free video call

  • Pick a free video app: Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime (Apple), or WhatsApp
  • Create a free account if the app asks for one
  • Ask the AI: “Write a short text message inviting my sister to a Zoom call next Tuesday at 2 p.m. Include the link once I have it.”
  • Create the meeting in the video app and copy the link
  • Paste the link into your message and send it
  • Test your camera and microphone before the call

Part 3: Organize family updates

  • Start a group chat with your close family (WhatsApp, Messenger, or iMessage)
  • Use AI to write a weekly update: “Write a short family update about my garden harvest and the doctor's appointment next week. Keep it upbeat.”
  • Paste the update into the group chat
  • Ask family members to reply with their own news

Part 4: Share photos and memories

  • Choose one photo you want to share
  • Ask the AI: “I am sharing a photo of my rose garden. Write a warm caption for Facebook or email, two sentences long.”
  • Copy the caption with your photo
  • Ask a family member to show you how if you get stuck — that is perfectly okay

Example things to say to the AI

SituationTry typing
Thank-you note “Write a short thank-you note to my neighbor for bringing me soup when I was sick. Keep it warm and simple.”
Birthday invitation “Write a birthday party invitation for my 75th birthday. Include date, time, address, and ask people to RSVP by phone.”
Catching up “Help me write an email to an old friend I have not spoken to in five years. I want to say hello and suggest coffee.”
Video call reminder “Write a friendly reminder text for our family Zoom call this Sunday at 4 p.m.”

Safety reminders

Do not put passwords, bank account numbers, or Social Security numbers in an email draft or an AI chat.

If someone you do not know asks you to video chat, ask a trusted family member first.

AI can make mistakes. Double-check dates, phone numbers, and addresses before you send anything.
