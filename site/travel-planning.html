Travel Planning with Free AI

Choose destinations, build itineraries, find senior discounts, and make packing lists — you stay in control.

What you will do

  • Choose a destination that fits your budget and energy level
  • Build a day-by-day itinerary
  • Find senior discounts and accessibility info
  • Make a packing list
  • Keep travel documents organized

What you need

  • A device with internet and a web browser
  • A free AI account (Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or Claude)
  • A notebook or notes app
  • A calendar

Step-by-step checklist

Part 1: Pick a destination

  • Think about: how far you want to travel, your budget, the weather you like, and how much walking you are comfortable with
  • Ask the AI: “Suggest three easy senior-friendly trips within [drive/fly] distance of [your state]. Include why each is good for a relaxed pace.”
  • Pick one destination
  • Ask: “What is the best time of year to visit [destination] for mild weather and smaller crowds?”

Part 2: Build a day-by-day itinerary

  • Ask the AI: “Create a 5-day relaxed itinerary for [destination] for a retiree. Include one main activity per day, rest time, and easy walking.”
  • Ask: “Suggest senior discounts or free days at museums and attractions in [destination].”
  • Write the itinerary in your notebook or print it
  • Check each attraction's website yourself before you go — hours and prices change

Part 3: Book travel and places to stay

  • Ask the AI: “What should I look for when booking a senior-friendly hotel? List questions to ask before reserving.”
  • Call the hotel directly and ask about ground-floor rooms, elevators, and accessibility
  • Book flights or trains directly through the airline or train company's official website
  • Ask a trusted family member to review any booking before you pay

Part 4: Make a packing list

  • Ask the AI: “Make a packing list for a 5-day trip to [destination] in [season]. Include medicines, comfortable shoes, and travel documents.”
  • Add your medicines and any medical devices
  • Check items off as you pack them
  • Put a copy of the list in your suitcase

Part 5: Organize documents

  • Make a folder (paper or digital) with: ID, reservation confirmations, insurance card, medication list, emergency contacts
  • Ask the AI: “Write a short 'In case of emergency' note for my suitcase, including who to call and my main medicines.” (Use first name and phone numbers only — no Social Security number)
  • Leave a copy of your itinerary with a trusted person at home

Part 6: While you travel

  • Ask the AI: “I am visiting [destination]. Find three highly-rated restaurants within walking distance of [your hotel] that offer easy seating and senior menus.”
  • Ask: “Write a short phrase in [local language] I can use to ask for help politely.”

Example things to say to the AI

SituationTry typing
Destination ideas “Suggest three relaxing places to visit in Florida for a senior who likes beaches and gentle walking.”
Itinerary “Create a 4-day slow-paced itinerary for Washington, D.C., with one museum per day and plenty of rest breaks.”
Packing “Make a packing list for a week-long trip to Arizona in October, including medications and comfortable walking shoes.”
Travel phrase “How do I say 'Where is the nearest pharmacy?' in Spanish, written so an English speaker can say it?”

Safety reminders

Never enter your passport number, credit card number, or Social Security number into an AI chat.

Book through official airline, train, or hotel websites. Be wary of deals that seem too good to be true (see Scam Avoidance).

AI can invent attractions, prices, or phone numbers. Always confirm on the official website or by phone.

Carry a paper copy of your itinerary and emergency contacts.
