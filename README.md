# Free AI for Retirement

A beginner-friendly manual and static HTML site for using free AI in retirement:
staying connected, hobby projects, health paperwork, travel planning, and scam
avoidance. Large-print friendly, plain English, step-by-step checklists.

Published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026), founded
by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author. Dylan writes AI guides
"like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending. More
at dylanasdale.github.io.

## Topics

- **Staying Connected** &mdash; email, video calls, group messages, photo captions
- **Hobby Projects** &mdash; gardening, crafts, recipes, genealogy, chess
- **Health Paperwork** &mdash; health summaries, medication schedules, appointment trackers, doctor-visit prep (education only, not medical advice)
- **Travel Planning** &mdash; destinations, itineraries, senior discounts, packing lists
- **Scam Avoidance** &mdash; spotting AI voice scams, fake emails, and what to do if scammed

## How to use it

1. Open `site/index.html` in any web browser.
2. Use the "Text size" buttons at the top of any page to make the text bigger or smaller (starts at 19 px, large-print friendly).
3. Work through a guide's checklist one step at a time. There is no rush.
4. Pages can be printed from your browser (Ctrl+P / Command+P). Navigation and controls are hidden when printing.

## Who this is for

Beginners, especially retirees, who want plain-English, step-by-step help with
free AI tools. No prior AI or computer expertise is assumed beyond opening a web
browser.

## Safety

- Never share passwords, Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, or your full home address with an AI tool.
- The health paperwork guide is education only &mdash; not medical advice. Always talk with your doctor or nurse about health decisions.
- See the Scam Avoidance guide for how to spot AI-generated scams.
