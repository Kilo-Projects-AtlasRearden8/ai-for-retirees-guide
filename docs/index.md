# Free AI for Retirement: A Beginner's Manual

Welcome. This guide shows you how to use free artificial intelligence (AI) tools
in everyday retirement life. You do not need to be a computer expert. You do
not need to spend money. Each guide below is written in plain English, with
step-by-step checklists you can follow at your own pace.

## Who this is for

This manual was written for beginners - especially retirees who want to stay
connected with family, enjoy hobbies, organize paperwork, plan travel, and avoid
scams. It assumes you know how to turn on a computer, tablet, or phone and open
a web browser. That is all.

## What is AI, in plain words

AI is a computer program that can read, write, summarize, and answer questions
almost like a person would. You type (or speak) a request, and the AI replies.
Think of it as a helpful assistant who never gets tired and does not judge you
for asking the same question twice.

The free tools in this manual work in your web browser or as free apps. You do
not need to install anything complicated.

## How to use each guide

Every topic guide follows the same pattern:

1. **What you will do** - a short summary in plain words
2. **What you need** - a device, an internet connection, and a free account
3. **Step-by-step checklist** - check off each step as you go
4. **Example things to say** - ready-to-copy phrases you can paste into the AI
5. **Safety reminders** - what not to share and when to ask a trusted person for help

## Safety first: a quick rule before you start

Never share passwords, Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, or your
full home address with an AI tool. Treat an AI like a helpful stranger: useful
for ideas and drafting, but not a place for secrets. The Scam Avoidance guide
explains this in more detail.

**Health note:** The health paperwork guide helps you organize your own
records. It is not medical advice. Always talk with your doctor or nurse about
health decisions.
