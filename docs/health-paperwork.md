# Organizing Health Paperwork with Free AI

Use free AI to sort, summarize, and keep track of your own medical records,
insurance forms, and appointment notes. This guide helps you organize
paperwork you already have. It is **not** medical advice.

## Important: this is not medical advice

AI cannot diagnose, prescribe, or replace your doctor or nurse. Always talk
with your healthcare team before making health decisions. If something in this
guide conflicts with what your care team says, follow your care team.

## What you will do

- Gather your health papers in one place
- Create a simple health summary for yourself
- Track medications and appointments
- Prepare questions for doctor visits
- Understand insurance forms (with help from a trusted person)

## What you need

- Your existing health papers (after-visit summaries, medication lists,
  insurance letters)
- A folder or binder, or a notes app on your phone/computer
- A free AI account (Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or Claude)
- A trusted family member or friend to review anything important

## Step-by-step checklist

### Part 1: Gather your papers

☐ Find one box, folder, or drawer for health papers  
☐ Collect: recent doctor visit summaries, medication lists, insurance cards,
  vaccination records, lab results  
☐ Sort them into piles: "recent," "insurance," "medications," "other"  
☐ Throw away junk mail and old expired coupons (shred anything with your
  name or birth date on it)  

### Part 2: Make a personal health summary

☐ Open your AI tool  
☐ Ask: *"Help me write a one-page health summary. I will give you my
  information bit by bit. Do not give medical advice - just organize what I
  say."*  
☐ Type in: your name (first name only is fine), date of birth (optional),
  main health conditions, surgeries, and allergies  
☐ Ask the AI to format it as a simple list  
☐ Copy the summary into a notes app or print it  
☐ Keep a copy in your wallet or purse for emergencies  

**Do not type your full Social Security number or insurance member ID into the
AI.** Use placeholders like [SSN] or [Member ID] instead.

### Part 3: Track medications

☐ List every medicine you take, the dose, and when you take it  
☐ Ask the AI: *"Make a simple medication schedule from this list. Use a
  table with morning, noon, evening, and bedtime."*  
☐ Copy the schedule onto paper and put it near where you take your pills  
☐ Show the schedule to your pharmacist or doctor to double-check it  

### Part 4: Track appointments

☐ Write down upcoming appointments: doctor, dentist, eye doctor, therapy  
☐ Ask the AI: *"Write a simple appointment tracker I can fill in. Include
  date, provider, reason, and what to bring."*  
☐ Print it and keep it on your refrigerator or by the phone  

### Part 5: Prepare for a doctor visit

☐ Before your appointment, write down 3 to 5 questions you want to ask  
☐ Ask the AI: *"Help me turn these notes into clear questions for my doctor.
  Keep them short."*  
☐ Bring the questions with you, or ask the nurse to read them  
☐ After the visit, ask: *"Help me write down what the doctor said in simple
  words."* (Type only what the doctor told you, not private ID numbers)  

### Part 6: Understand insurance forms

☐ If a bill or insurance letter confuses you, show it to a trusted family
   member first  
☐ Ask the AI: *"Explain what an 'Explanation of Benefits' form is in simple
  words, and what I should look for."*  
☐ Never upload a photo of your insurance card to an AI tool  
☐ Call the phone number on your insurance card if you have questions

## Example things to say to the AI

| Situation | Try typing |
|-----------|------------|
| Health summary | "Organize this into a simple health summary: high blood pressure, takes lisinopril 10 mg each morning, allergic to penicillin." |
| Medication schedule | "Make a daily schedule: lisinopril 10 mg at 8 a.m., atorvastatin 20 mg at bedtime, aspirin 81 mg after breakfast." |
| Doctor questions | "I am seeing my doctor about knee pain. Write 4 simple questions to ask about treatment options." |
| Visit notes | "Turn this into plain English: 'Patient has osteoarthritis, continue physical therapy, follow up in 3 months.'" |

## Safety reminders

- **Never** type your Social Security number, full insurance member ID,
  credit card number, or complete home address into an AI chat.
- Use a paper folder for original documents. Treat the AI copy as a helper,
  not the official record.
- Ask a trusted person to review anything about bills, insurance, or new
  diagnoses before you act on it.
- Keep your health summary somewhere easy to find in an emergency.
