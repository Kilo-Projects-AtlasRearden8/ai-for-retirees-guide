# Hobby Projects with Free AI

Use free AI to enjoy your hobbies more - gardening, crafts, cooking, genealogy,
games, and anything else you love. AI is good at ideas, lists, and explaining
things step by step.

## What you will do

- Get hobby ideas matched to your interests and energy level
- Get simple project instructions
- Organize recipes, patterns, and notes
- Research family history
- Learn games like chess at your own pace

## What you need

- A device with internet and a web browser
- A free AI account (Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or Claude)
- A notebook or a notes app on your phone (optional but helpful)

## Step-by-step checklist

### Part 1: Pick a hobby and get ideas

☐ Think of one thing you enjoy or want to try  
☐ Ask the AI: *"I am retired and enjoy [gardening/knitting/birdwatching].
  Suggest three easy hobby projects I can start this month with supplies I
  probably already have."*  
☐ Pick one project that sounds fun  
☐ Write it down in your notebook  

### Part 2: Get step-by-step instructions

☐ Ask the AI: *"Give me simple step-by-step instructions for [your project].
  Write it for a beginner. Use short sentences and number the steps."*  
☐ Read the steps one at a time  
☐ Do step 1, then step 2. Check each one off as you finish  
☐ If a step is confusing, ask: *"Can you explain step 3 in an even simpler
  way?"*  

### Part 3: Organize your hobby notes

☐ Open a notes app or a paper notebook  
☐ Ask the AI: *"Make a simple checklist of supplies I need for [project].
  Group them by where I might buy them."*  
☐ Copy the list and check items off as you gather them  
☐ Save finished project notes so you can repeat them next year  

### Part 4: Recipe and cooking help

☐ Ask the AI: *"I have [chicken, potatoes, and carrots]. Suggest an easy
  dinner recipe with step-by-step instructions."*  
☐ Ask for changes you need: *"Make it low-salt"* or *"I do not like onions,
  what can I use instead?"*  
☐ Print or copy the recipe and follow it while cooking  

### Part 5: Family history and genealogy

☐ Write down what you already know: names, birthplaces, approximate dates  
☐ Ask the AI: *"Help me organize what I know about my family into a simple
  family tree outline. Suggest what questions to ask older relatives."*  
☐ Interview a relative and write down their stories  
☐ Ask the AI: *"Turn these notes into a short family story I can share with
  my grandchildren."*  

### Part 6: Games and puzzles

☐ Ask the AI: *"Teach me how to play chess. Explain how each piece moves,
  using simple words."*  
☐ Set up a board or use a free chess website  
☐ Play one move at a time. Ask the AI: *"What is a good next move? Explain
  why."*  

## Example things to say to the AI

| Situation | Try typing |
|-----------|------------|
| Gardening | "I have a sunny backyard and want to grow tomatoes. Give me a beginner's guide, step by step." |
| Crafts | "Suggest an easy knitting project for a beginner. List the yarn size and needle size I need." |
| Recipes | "I want to bake cookies with my grandchild. Give me a simple recipe with only five ingredients." |
| Genealogy | "My grandfather was born in Indiana around 1920. How can I start finding records about him?" |
| Chess | "Explain how a knight moves in chess, with an example." |

## Safety reminders

- Hobby AI chats are public-ish. Do not share your home address, phone number,
  or financial details.
- If you order supplies online, use a credit card with fraud protection and
  check the website is real (see the Scam Avoidance guide).
- AI may give wrong facts about history or health. Double-check important
  information.
